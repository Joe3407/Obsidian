## Door 1

- **Economics:** It's the study of how individuals and societies choose to allocate scarce resources, why they choose to allocate them that way, and the consequences of those decisions
- **Scarcity:** Resources which are limited relative to people’s wants/needs, eg. Caviar, Nice mountain view, Clothes made from fabric 
- **Four Factors of Production:** Land, Labor, Capital (Tools that are used to produce products), Entrepreneurship (Technology, Brain that thinks where and how to put the previous factors)
- **Statements:** 
	- **Normative:** Can't be tested, based on morals and opinions 
	- **Positive:** Can be tested, based on right and wrong 
- **Economic Model:** It's a simplified representation of a real-world situation that helps economists understand or predict what happens

- **SMEs** are small and medium-sized businesses that often need financing to grow. However, many SMEs are already carrying debt and are hesitant to take on more loans, while banks also see lending to small businesses as relatively risky. The article argues that banks should support SMEs not only through financing but also through services such as mentorship and business guidance

- **Commercial Bank:** General banking: deposits + loans + payments, deals with costumers 
- **Central Bank:** Deals with banks

## Door 2

| Basis         | Services                                                                                     | Goods                                                                                          |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Intangibility | Intangible i.e. Services cannot be touched. They do not have physical existence.             | Tangible i.e. Goods can be touched. They have physical existence.                              |
| Inconsistency | Different customers have different demands and expectations.                                 | Physical goods are produced with a high degree of standardization.                             |
| Inseparability| Services cannot be produced now for consumption later on e.g. Services of a Doctor           | Separation of production and consumption e.g. TV manufactured in Japan and sold in India.      |
| Inventory     | Cannot be kept in stock e.g. Unoccupied seats in airlines, theatre or hotels.                | Can be kept in stock e.g. TV, mobile phones, computers, sugar, wheat etc.                      |
| Involvement   | Customers participate in the production.                                                     | Customers do not participate in production.                                                    |
| Nature        | An activity or process e.g. watching a movie in a cinema hall                                | A physical object e.g. Table, Chair, Fan etc.                                                  |
| Type          | Heterogeneous                                                                                | Homogenous                                                                                     |
| Ownership     | No transfer of ownership
- **Types of Services:**
	- **Business:**  Banking, Insurance, Transportation
	- **Social:** Improve the standard of living for weaker sections of society 
	- **Personal:** Tourism, Restaurants
- **Bank:**
	- **Meaning:** It's a financial institution licensed to receive deposits and give loans 
	- **Types:**
		- **Central:** Supervises, Controls, and Regulates the activities of all the commercial banks 
		- **Commercial:** General banking: deposits + loans + payments, deals with costumers
		- **Cooperative:** It's members are the owners and customers of the bank 
		- **Specialized:** They are created for special purpose 
		- **Retail:** Making the normal transactions of a bank but with individuals not huge companies 
- **Types of cheques:** 
	- Bearer = whoever bears/holds it → can cash it 
	- Crossed = goes through the bank → more secure

- **Types of Bank Accounts:**
	- **Saving Deposit:** Small savings of household 
	- **Current Deposit:** Can deposit money any number of times, and can withdraw money several times a day 
	- **Recurring Deposit:** Total amount is repaid with interest at the end of the particular fixed period
	- **Fixed Deposit:** Money is accepted for a fixed period, the longer the period the higher the rate of interest 

- **e-Banking:** Online Banking 
- Paypal, wise, payoneer
